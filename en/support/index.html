Need help with f/8 Portfolio? The answers to the most common questions are below. If yours isn't here, ask us at github.com/AJ-Software-LTD/f8-legal/issues and we'll get back to you. Questions there are public, so please don't include personal details or photos.

Common questions

  • f/8 doesn't show my photos. f/8 needs access to your Photos library. On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Photos, and choose Full Access for f/8 Portfolio. On a Mac, go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Photos, and turn on f/8 Portfolio.
  • Backing up or sharing a tag doesn't work. f/8 keeps tags in your own iCloud, so your device must be signed in to iCloud and online. If you're signed in, check that iCloud is turned on for f/8 Portfolio in your Apple Account settings.
  • Someone can't see the tag I shared. They need f/8 Portfolio on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, signed in to iCloud with the Apple Account for the email address you invited.
  • I bought Pro on another device. Open f/8 Pro in f/8 and choose Restore Purchases. Pro works on every device signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • How do I cancel Pro? Choose Manage Subscription on the f/8 Pro screen, or open your subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • I saw something that breaks the rules. Open the tag's menu and choose Report, or block its owner. We review every report, and can take a tag down for everyone.

Your data

f/8 has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Read how it handles your photos in the Privacy Policy, and the rules for using it in the Terms of Use.
