f/8 Portfolio Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026
Terms of Use

f/8 has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never sells anything about you. Your photos stay on your Mac unless you choose to put a tag in iCloud, and what you put in iCloud is stored in your own iCloud account, not on servers we run.

Who we are

f/8 Portfolio ("f/8") is made by AJ Software Ltd ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here, under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can contact us about anything in this policy at github.com/built-by-aj/f8-legal/issues.

On your Mac

  • Photos. With your permission, f/8 reads the photos you choose from your Photos library or from folders on your Mac. If a photo is stored in iCloud Photos, macOS may download it for f/8.
  • Photo details. To tag your photos, f/8 reads each photo's details: the date it was taken, its location, its size and format, the camera and lens, the focal length, aperture, exposure and ISO, and whether it's a favourite, Portrait, HDR, Live or panorama photo.
  • Place names. To turn a photo's location into a place name such as "London", f/8 sends the photo's coordinates to Apple's location service, which returns the city, region and country. f/8 doesn't use Location Services and never asks where you are.
  • What f/8 keeps. Your tags, the details above, your settings, any music you add to a slideshow, and copies of photos shared with you are stored only on your Mac, in f/8's own folder. Copies of other people's photos are encrypted, with a key kept in your Mac's keychain.
  • Sensitive photos. If you've turned on Sensitive Content Warning in macOS, f/8 uses Apple's on-device analysis to blur photos that may contain nudity. The analysis happens entirely on your Mac and nothing is sent anywhere.

In iCloud

When you put a tag in iCloud, f/8 stores it in your own private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. We can't see it. It counts towards your iCloud storage, and Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy apply. f/8 stores:

  • the photos, at the quality you choose. Exact locations are always removed. At Reduced Quality, every other detail is removed too. At Original Quality, camera details such as the camera, lens and settings are kept.
  • the photos' details from f/8, without coordinates, including the place name.
  • the tag's name, order, quality and slideshow settings.
  • the name of each Mac you upload from, kept apart from your tags and never shared, so your other Macs can show where a tag came from under "From Your Other Macs".

Sharing with people you invite (f/8 Pro)

When you share a tag, the people you invite can see:

  • its photos
  • its name
  • its cover image and slideshow
  • the name you share under: your first name, or a name you choose in Settings › Sharing. f/8 never shares your full name.
  • the other people invited to it, as Apple's iCloud shows them to each other

f/8 records who may download and who's invited to watch a slideshow in a scrambled (hashed) form, so these lists don't reveal anyone's email address.

To invite someone, you enter their email address. Apple uses it to find their iCloud account and add them to the share. f/8 then:

  • Leaves an invitation in f/8's shared mailbox, a public CloudKit database run by Apple for f/8. The invitation contains a scrambled (hashed) form of their email address, your sharing name, the tag's name, the number of photos and the share link. Their copy of f/8 finds the invitation and shows a notification. Invitations are removed when you withdraw them or remove the tag from iCloud.
  • Emails the invitation. If f/8's invitation email service is on, f/8 sends their email address, your sharing name, the tag's name, the number of photos, whether they may download, your note and the share link to that service. It runs on Cloudflare and sends the email through Resend. It briefly uses your internet (IP) address to prevent abuse, and it doesn't store any of this after the email is sent. If the service is off, f/8 opens an email in your own mail app for you to send instead.

f/8 keeps a list of the people who've accepted your invitations on your Mac, so you can invite them again easily.

Public links (f/8 Pro)

If you make a tag public, anyone with the link can view it in f/8 on a Mac signed in to iCloud. They see its photos, name, cover image and slideshow, and your sharing name, but not who you've invited. Nobody can download from a public link. You'll see how many people have opened it. Share a public link only where you're happy for anyone to see it.

Reports and moderation

Anyone who can see a shared or public tag can report it. A report is stored in f/8's public CloudKit database and contains:

  • the reporter's iCloud identifier, an anonymous ID that Apple creates for f/8
  • the tag's owner (their iCloud identifier and name)
  • the tag's name and link
  • the reason and any note the reporter writes

Only f/8's moderators can read reports. The person reported isn't told who reported them.

If a tag breaks the rules in our Terms of Use, we can take it down, which hides it in every copy of f/8. If someone keeps breaking the rules, we can take down all of their tags. A record of each takedown, with the owner's iCloud identifier, is kept in the public database so it can be applied everywhere. The photos themselves stay in the owner's iCloud.

Where the law requires it, such as for sexual content involving children, we report content to the police or the relevant authorities. When you block someone, that's stored only on your Mac.

Purchases

f/8 Pro is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details, and f/8 checks your subscription with Apple on your Mac. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple delivers them to f/8 when someone invites you to a tag.

What f/8 doesn't do

  • f/8 doesn't use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party tracking.
  • It doesn't read your contacts, use Location Services, or access your camera or microphone.
  • Music you add to a slideshow stays on your Mac and is never shared. Shared slideshows can only use f/8's own public-domain tracks.

Why we use your data

  • To give you the features you ask for: tagging, iCloud, sharing, invitations, public links and Pro. This is necessary to provide f/8 to you (the performance of a contract).
  • To keep f/8 safe: reports, takedowns and preventing abuse of the invitation service. This is our legitimate interest, and that of everyone who uses f/8, in a safe service.
  • To meet legal obligations: for example, reporting illegal content.

Who else handles your data

We use these companies to run f/8. Each handles your data under its own privacy terms:

  • Apple: iCloud and CloudKit, place names, the App Store and notifications.
  • Cloudflare and Resend: the invitation email service, if it's on.

These companies may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. They use recognised safeguards, such as the UK–US data bridge or standard contractual clauses. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

  • On your Mac: until you delete it or delete f/8's data.
  • In iCloud: until you remove the tag from iCloud in f/8 (Cloud › Remove from iCloud), or delete f/8's data in System Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Deleting f/8 from your Mac doesn't remove what's in iCloud.
  • Invitations: until they're withdrawn, or the tag is removed from iCloud.
  • Reports: for up to 2 years after we've dealt with them, so we can act on repeated breaches, unless we need them longer for legal reasons.
  • Takedown records: for as long as the takedown applies.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data
  • have it corrected or deleted
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • have it moved to another service

Most of your data is in your own iCloud account and on your Mac, where you control it directly. For anything else, contact us at github.com/built-by-aj/f8-legal/issues. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

f/8 isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. You must be at least 13 to share tags or make them public.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If the changes are significant, such as f/8 moving your photos to storage we run, we'll tell you in f/8 first.
